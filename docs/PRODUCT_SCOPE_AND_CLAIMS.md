# Public Product Scope, Claims, and Confidentiality Rules

## Publicly safe to communicate
The following are supported by the current project direction:
- EMIQEN is a developing voice-and-text AI assistant concept.
- It focuses on selected enterprise language tasks.
- Indian English, Indian-context terminology, and contextual interpretation are key design goals.
- The planned system can combine speech recognition, language processing, context handling, response checks, and speech generation.
- Emotion-aware interaction is an area of planned development.
- The architecture is intended to be modular.
- The team intends to evaluate controlled/private deployment options.

Use forward-looking language for capabilities that have not been verified in a working demo.

## Do not publish
Do not place any of the following on the public website:
- Pricing, per-token rates, cloud bills, GPU rental rates, hardware purchase budgets, cost-per-turn calculations, financial projections, or internal cost comparisons.
- Detailed AWS architecture, account/region details, instance types, infrastructure topology, scaling plans, deployment diagrams, database schemas, internal endpoints, or operational runbooks.
- API keys, credentials, secrets, internal URLs, access tokens, or private contact information.
- Exact model parameter counts, training compute estimates, training schedules, optimizer/configuration details, internal model dimensions, training-token targets, checkpoint strategy, or unpublished benchmark results.
- Dataset inventory, dataset sizes, download links, corpus composition, private vocabulary mappings, training examples that reveal internal data, or data acquisition strategy.
- Internal project proposal/DPR, risk register, implementation timeline, procurement notes, internal staffing/module division, or unresolved design decisions.
- Unapproved customer names, logos, testimonials, partnerships, endorsements, compliance certifications, security guarantees, or service-level commitments.
- Unapproved product pricing, plan names, launch dates, or availability statements.

## Claims discipline
- Do not imply EMIQEN already has a fully trained or production-ready proprietary SLM.
- Do not state that all modules are integrated or validated unless confirmed by the project owner.
- Do not promise “human-level”, “perfect”, “unbiased”, “zero hallucinations”, “guaranteed accuracy”, “real-time” or a numerical latency/uptime result.
- Do not claim that self-hosting automatically guarantees privacy or regulatory compliance.
- Do not claim a specific cost saving without a reproducible benchmark and an approved comparison methodology.
- Do not claim full support for all Indian languages. Mention Indian English and Indian-context language handling as a focus; specify languages only after they are actually supported and tested.
- Emotion detection is probabilistic; do not frame inferred emotion as a definitive psychological fact.
- Do not present the IMDb-like/movie-review website as the main commercial product unless the owner explicitly changes the positioning. Keep the public message focused on enterprise voice-and-text AI.

## Suggested safe wording
Prefer:
- “is being developed to…”
- “designed to support…”
- “the team is evaluating…”
- “planned capability”
- “selected enterprise workflows”
- “Indian-context language handling”

Avoid:
- “delivers industry-leading accuracy”
- “reduces cost by X%”
- “runs entirely on-premises today”
- “supports every Indian language”
- “guarantees secure/private processing”
- “available now” unless the product is publicly launched.

## Approval checklist before launch
- Confirm product status and the capabilities available in the current build.
- Confirm official domain and approved contact channel.
- Confirm logo files and usage rights.
- Confirm privacy policy and terms requirements before collecting user data.
- Confirm consent language before recording microphone input.
- Confirm analytics/cookie behavior and any third-party services.
- Review all claims, forms, links, and accessibility.
