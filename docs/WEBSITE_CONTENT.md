EMIQEN is being developed as a voice-and-text AI assistant for enterprise workflows, with a focus on domain-specific language tasks and the nuances of Indian English and everyday Indian business communication.

## Section: The challenge
**Heading:** Business language is never one-size-fits-all.

**Copy:** Enterprise conversations are full of domain terminology, regional expressions, different accents, and context that generic systems may miss. Teams need AI that can interpret requests consistently and support focused tasks without adding unnecessary complexity.

Three concise cards:
1. **Context gets lost** — Local expressions and business-specific terminology can change the meaning of a request.
2. **Workflows need focus** — Many enterprise tasks are narrow and repetitive, from summarizing information to extracting key details.
3. **Voice and text should work together** — Teams and customers should be able to interact in the format that suits the moment.

## Section: What EMIQEN is building
**Heading:** One experience for voice, text, and context.

**Intro:** EMIQEN is designed around a modular AI workflow that can bring together:

- **Voice and text interaction** — Give users a choice of speaking or typing.
- **Domain-focused responses** — Support selected enterprise tasks such as summarization, classification, information extraction, and question answering.
- **Indian-context language handling** — Interpret relevant Indian English usage, local terminology, and contextual vocabulary.
- **Emotion-aware interaction** — Explore how signals from text and voice can help shape a more appropriate response.
- **Natural spoken responses** — Provide voice output where the use case calls for it.
- **Adaptable deployment** — Evaluate deployment options to meet an organisation’s operational and data-handling needs.

Do not present each item as production-ready unless the project team confirms it has been implemented and tested.

## Section: How it works
**Heading:** From conversation to useful response.

Use a simple four-step visual:
1. **Speak or type** — A user asks a question or makes a request.
2. **Understand the input** — The system processes the query and, where applicable, speech and contextual signals.
3. **Generate and adapt** — The language layer produces a task-focused response, with Indian-context processing where relevant.
4. **Respond in the right format** — Return text and, when enabled, spoken output.

Use a simple diagram or four connected cards. Keep model names, internal service boundaries, and training details out of this public section.

## Section: Designed for practical workflows
**Heading:** Built around work that needs to get done.

Use examples as illustrative use cases, not guaranteed product features:

- **Knowledge and document workflows** — Explore summarization and information extraction for enterprise information.
- **Internal assistants** — Support employees with focused questions and repeatable tasks.
- **Voice-enabled services** — Make selected workflows accessible through spoken interaction.

Include a note: *Use cases are illustrative. Suitability and availability depend on implementation and evaluation.*

## Section: Why EMIQEN
**Heading:** A focused approach to enterprise AI.

- **Indian context at the centre** — Designed to account for Indian English, local vocabulary, and context-sensitive expressions.
- **Purpose-built direction** — Focused on selected enterprise workloads rather than claiming to solve every task.
- **Voice and text together** — A single product direction for typed and spoken interactions.
- **Modular by design** — Components can be evaluated and improved independently.
- **Deployment flexibility as a design goal** — Deployment choices will be assessed against customer needs and validated capabilities.

Avoid claims such as “best”, “most accurate”, “lowest cost”, “zero hallucinations”, “fully private”, or “production-ready” unless independently substantiated and approved.
